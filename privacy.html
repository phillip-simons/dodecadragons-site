This policy covers the DodecaDragons app for iPhone and iPad ("the app"). The short version: your game stays on your device, the developer doesn't collect anything about you, and the free version shows banner ads from Google.

Your game data

Your save, your settings (such as haptics and confirmation toggles) and whether you've left a tip are stored only on your device. The app has no account and doesn't send your game anywhere. If you copy or share an export string, you choose where it goes. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

Unless you've left a tip, the app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's software may collect or receive information such as your device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, an app-specific device identifier, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

The app doesn't ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) isn't available to Google through the app.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before requesting ads, and you can change your choice later under Settings → Ad privacy options in the app.

Learn how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.

Tips and purchases

Tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store whether a tip was purchased, so it can turn off the ads and restore that on your other devices.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and the developer doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
